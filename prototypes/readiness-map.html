Method

How the index is built

  1. Each indicator is rescaled to 0–100 against a fixed floor and ceiling, so a score means the same thing every year.
  2. Indicators where less is better, such as the pupil–teacher ratio, are inverted.
  3. A domain score is the mean of its indicators.
  4. The composite is the weighted mean of the six domains.

The known flaw. A mean hides a zero. A state can look mid-table while failing one indicator completely. The distance-to-target view exists to expose that.
